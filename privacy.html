Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Who uses it

Ariadne has exactly one user: its developer, who organizes their own Google Drive with it. It offers no accounts and accepts no other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent, Ariadne reads Google Drive file metadata (names, types, sizes, dates, folder structure and content checksums) and moves, renames or trashes files in that same Drive when the owner approves. It does not read file contents to profile anyone.

Where data goes

Nowhere. Everything Ariadne learns stays on the owner's own computer. The sign-in token is stored encrypted on that computer. No data is sold, shared, sent to third parties or used for advertising, and nothing is transferred to any server operated by the developer.

Google API Services

Ariadne's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removal

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; deleting Ariadne's local folder removes everything it stored.
